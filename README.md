## gdr2re4
Convert between GDH's re4 replay format to .gdr (eclipse's default) and vice versa

Usage:
- Take your .gdr file, drag it to the .exe, and it'll spit out the .re4
or
- Take your .re4 file, drag it to the .exe, and it'll spit out the .gdr

### Features:
- Preserves TPS
- can also be used as CLI (do `gdr2re4.exe --help`)
